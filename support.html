Support

Stuck on something? The answer is probably here. If not, write to us.

How does the free trial work?

Your first 3 games are free. A game counts once it reaches the results screen, in the Journey or in Just Play. After that, a one-time purchase unlocks the full game for good and adds 100 Encores.

I bought the full game. How do I get it on a new device?

Open Music Tap, start a game to bring up the unlock screen, and tap Restore Purchase. Make sure you're signed in with the same Apple Account you bought it with.

What are Encores?

When you miss a tile, an Encore lets you continue right where you stopped. Runs that use Encores don't count for the Game Center leaderboards. Your Encores are stored on your device, so they don't move to a new device and are lost if you delete the app.

How do I play my own songs?

Go to My Music and tap +, then pick an audio file from Files. Music Tap listens to the song and writes the tiles on your device; this can take a moment for long songs. Songs protected by DRM, like Apple Music downloads, can't be imported.

The game feels too fast or too hard.

Every song comes in Easy, Normal and Hard, and each level speeds the song up a little. Start in the Easy world of the Journey; a stop is cleared with just one star.

Leaderboards say "Sign in"

Rankings use Game Center. Sign in under Settings → Game Center on your device, then open Music Tap again. You can always play without Game Center.

Can I get a refund?

Purchases are handled by Apple. You can request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com.
