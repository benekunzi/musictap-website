Privacy Policy

Effective 19 November 2026

Music Tap is a rhythm game for iPhone and iPad. In short: we don't collect any personal data. There is no account, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking. This policy explains what the app does with information on your device.

Information we collect

None. Music Tap has no servers of its own and does not send your data anywhere. We never see who you are, what you play or how you play.

What stays on your device

  • Progress and scores — your Journey progress, best scores, Encore balance and settings are stored only inside the app on your device. Deleting the app deletes them.
  • Your own music — when you import a song, Music Tap analyses it and creates the tiles right on your device. The file and its tiles stay on your device and are never uploaded.

Game Center

If you choose to sign in to Game Center, your scores are sent to Apple to show leaderboards. Game Center is run by Apple under Apple's Privacy Policy; we don't receive your Apple ID or contact details. You can play without Game Center.

Purchases

The full game unlock and Encores are bought through the Apple App Store. Apple handles the payment; we never receive your payment details. The app only checks with Apple whether a purchase was made.

This website

This website is hosted by Vercel Inc. (USA). When you open a page, Vercel processes your IP address and basic request data (time, page, browser) in short-lived server logs, so the site can be delivered and protected against abuse (legitimate interest, Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). Vercel is certified under the EU–US Data Privacy Framework. The site uses no cookies, no analytics and no third-party fonts or scripts.

When you email us

If you write to us, we use your email address and message only to answer you (Art. 6(1)(b) and (f) GDPR) and delete them once your request is settled, unless the law requires us to keep them.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to access, correct or delete your personal data, to restrict or object to its processing, and to data portability. You can also complain to a data protection supervisory authority. Since the app itself doesn't collect personal data, there is usually nothing to access or delete; for Game Center and purchases, contact Apple.

Children

Music Tap doesn't collect personal data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, we'll update this page and the date above.
